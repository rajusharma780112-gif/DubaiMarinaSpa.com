What it involves

Swedish massage uses long, gliding strokes across the full body at moderate, consistent pressure. It's designed to relax the whole nervous system rather than target one specific area.

Who it's for

A good choice for a first visit, or when you want to properly switch off rather than work through a specific problem area. Popular with JVC guests who've had a stressful week and just want to unwind.

What to expect

Sessions typically run 60 or 90 minutes. Pressure stays gentle to moderate throughout — tell your therapist if you'd like it firmer or lighter at any point.

Common Questions

Booking Swedish Massage.

Is Swedish massage the same as relaxation massage?

Yes, it's often used interchangeably — Swedish is the technique behind most general relaxation massage.

Can I request firmer pressure during a Swedish massage?

Yes, though if you want consistently firm pressure throughout, deep tissue massage may suit you better — let us know when booking.
