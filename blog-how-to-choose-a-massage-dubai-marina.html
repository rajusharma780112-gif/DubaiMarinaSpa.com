How to Choose the Right
Massage in Dubai Marina.

Published 10 June 2026 · Updated 30 July 2026

Dubai Marina spas offer a wide range of massage styles, and picking the wrong one can leave you disappointed — too gentle when you wanted deep pressure, or too intense for a first visit. Here's how to actually choose.

Swedish / Relaxation Massage

Long, flowing strokes at moderate pressure. Best if your main goal is unwinding after a stressful week rather than fixing a specific muscle problem — this is the most common first-time booking in the Marina.

Deep Tissue Massage

Firmer, slower strokes that target deeper muscle layers. Good for chronic tension in the shoulders and lower back, or recovery after workouts. Expect some soreness the next day, similar to a workout.

Moroccan Bath (Hammam)

A steam and exfoliation ritual rather than a traditional massage — popular for guests wanting a full-body reset rather than muscle-specific work. Usually takes longer than a standard massage session.

Couples Massage

Two therapists, same room, side by side — a popular choice for anniversaries and date nights in the Marina. Worth booking a few days ahead since side-by-side rooms are limited at most spas.

Still not sure?

Mention what you're after in the message field when you get matched, and we'll steer you toward a spa and therapist suited to it — including whether you want a male or female therapist, which most Marina spas can accommodate with advance notice.
