How Often Should
You Get a Massage?

It depends on the goal

There's no single right answer — it depends on whether you're managing chronic tension, recovering from activity, or just want regular relaxation. Each of these calls for a different rhythm.

For chronic tension

If you're dealing with ongoing tightness — desk-related shoulder tension is the most common example — every 2 to 4 weeks tends to keep it from building back up between sessions. Less frequent than that, and tension often returns to baseline before the next visit.

For general relaxation

If it's about stress relief rather than a specific physical issue, monthly is a common rhythm — enough to notice a difference without needing to prioritise it too heavily against everything else.

For active recovery

If you train regularly, timing matters more than frequency — a sports massage the day after a hard session tends to be more useful than a fixed weekly schedule regardless of training load.

Signs you should increase frequency

If tension consistently returns within a week of your last session, or if a specific area keeps flaring up, that's usually a sign to tighten the interval rather than switch technique.
