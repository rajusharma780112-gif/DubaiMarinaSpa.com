Common Questions

Before you
get matched.

Is this an actual spa?

No — we're an independent matching service. We connect you with vetted, independently-run spas in Dubai Marina; treatments happen at their premises.

Does it cost anything to use?

Nothing. You only pay the spa's normal treatment price when you visit — our matching service is free for guests.

How fast will I hear back?

Most guests hear from a matched spa within 15 minutes during the day. Late-night requests are confirmed the next morning.

Can I request a specific spa?

Yes — mention it in the message field and we'll try to match you there if they're part of our network and available.

Are the spas licensed?

Every spa in our network is checked for a valid trade licence and hygiene standards before we add them.

What if I'm not happy with the match?

Message us and we'll match you with a different spa — no charge, no questions asked.
