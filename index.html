Massage

Three styles,
one focus.

We specialise in massage only — no facials, no nails, just massage done well.

Swedish

Long, flowing strokes for full-body relaxation — our most-booked session. Good for a first visit or when you just want to properly switch off, rather than target a specific problem area.

Deep Tissue

Firm, targeted pressure for chronic tension in the neck, shoulders and back. Best suited to guests with ongoing tightness from desk work or stress, rather than general relaxation.

Sports

Pre- or post-workout massage focused on recovery and mobility. Popular with JVC's gym-goers and runners around the community's parks and tracks.
