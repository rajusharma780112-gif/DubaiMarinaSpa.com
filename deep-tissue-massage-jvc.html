What it involves

Deep tissue massage uses slow, firm pressure to work through deeper layers of muscle, targeting chronic tension rather than general relaxation.

Who it's for

Best suited to guests with ongoing tightness from desk work, stress or an old injury — a common request from JVC's office-working residents.

What to expect

Sessions run 60 or 90 minutes. Pressure is noticeably firmer than a Swedish massage — speak up early if it ever feels like too much rather than just intense.

Common Questions

Booking Deep Tissue Massage.

Will deep tissue massage hurt?

It can feel intense, especially over particularly tight areas, but shouldn't be unbearable — tell your therapist if pressure ever feels wrong.

Is there soreness afterward?

Mild soreness for a day or so is normal, similar to post-workout soreness. Drinking water afterward tends to help.
