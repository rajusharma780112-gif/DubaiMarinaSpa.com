Swedish & Relaxation Massage in Dubai Marina.

Long, flowing strokes to ease tension after a long day around the Marina — matched with a vetted spa near you.

Request Swedish & Relaxation Massage Message on WhatsApp

What it involves

Swedish massage uses long, gliding strokes at moderate, consistent pressure across the full body, designed to relax the nervous system rather than target one specific area.

Who it's for

A good choice for a first visit, or when you want to properly switch off after a stressful week rather than work through a specific problem area — the most commonly requested treatment among Marina guests.

What to expect

Sessions typically run 60 or 90 minutes at your matched spa. Pressure stays gentle to moderate throughout — mention if you'd like it firmer or lighter when you book.

Common Questions

Booking Swedish & Relaxation Massage.

Is Swedish massage the same as a relaxation massage?

Yes, the terms are often used interchangeably — Swedish is the technique behind most general relaxation massage offered at Marina spas.

Can I request a specific spa for this treatment?

Yes — mention it in the message field when you request a match, and we'll try to place you there if they're in our network and available.
